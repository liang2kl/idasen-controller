# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 28, 2026_

Idasen does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- The app communicates only with your desk, over Bluetooth Low Energy, to read its height and send movement commands.
- Your presets and the identifier of your desk are stored locally on your Mac and never leave it.
- There are no accounts, analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs, and the app makes no network requests.

Questions? Open an issue at <https://github.com/liang2kl/idasen-controller/issues>.

---

# 隐私政策

_最后更新：2026 年 10 月 28 日_

Idasen 不收集、存储或分享任何个人数据。

- app 仅通过低功耗蓝牙与你的升降桌通信，用于读取高度和发送升降指令。
- 你的预设和升降桌标识符只保存在你的 Mac 本地，不会离开设备。
- 没有账户、统计分析、追踪、广告或第三方 SDK，app 不进行任何网络请求。

如有疑问，请在 <https://github.com/liang2kl/idasen-controller/issues> 提交 issue。
